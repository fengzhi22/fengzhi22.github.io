Bio

Fengzhi Zhu is a Ph.D. candidate in Accounting at the Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. My research focuses on narrative disclosure, accounting conservatism and corporate governance. My working papers investigate the asymetric responses to good and bad news in narrative disclosure, the sample biases in the textual analysis literature of finance and accounting, and the effect of voting requirement on firm disclosure in the M&A setting. I am a supporter of the open science ideology, striving to make research more transparent and replicable for the academia community and more understandable and interesting for the general public. I enjoy singing, dancing, climbing, playing tennis and video games in my free time. I received a Bachelor's Degree in Finance and Accounting in 2017 and a Master's Degree in Business and Finance in 2019, both from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.
