Bio

Fengzhi Zhu is a Ph.D. candidate in Accounting at the Universidad Carlos III de Madrid. Her research focuses on narrative disclosure, accounting conservatism and corporate social responsibility in corporate financial reporting. Latest working paper examines firms' level of accounting conservatism reflected in textual disclosure and its consistency with numeric disclosure. As a supporter of open science with special interest in coding, she likes collecting openly accessible data via internet and sharing analysis via data visualization tools. She enjoys business simulation and role-playing video games in free time. She received a Bachelor's Degree in Finance and Accounting in 2017 and a Master's Degree in Business and Finance in 2019, both from Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.
